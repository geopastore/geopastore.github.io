### Flute Marks
Flute marks are common sedimentary structures produced by the erosion of mud beds, when water currents are powerful enough to suspend a good amount of sand. We can observe oblate hollows similalry oriented, smoothly rounded and heel shaped preserved in the overtourned sandstone bed.
